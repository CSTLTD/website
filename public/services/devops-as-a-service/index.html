DevOps as a Service

In an ever-changing world of technology, speed and reliability are the keys to staying ahead. DevOps is no longer an option; it's a fundamental requirement. Our DevOps-as-a-Service is designed to unify your teams, streamline your processes, and enhance your products’ speed to market. We go beyond the traditional software development and infrastructure management services to provide a collaborative and efficient environment that paves the way for innovation and growth.

Harness the transformative power of DevOps with CST, a team of experts wholly dedicated to empowering organizations through innovative IT infrastructure enhancements and efficient automation strategies.

Our offerings go beyond the typical DevOps services, covering:

  • Tooling and IT Infrastructure Management

  • Infrastructure as Code (IaC) Implementation using Terraform, Ansible, Cluster-API, and Crossplane

  • Comprehensive Configuration Management

  • Advanced Linux Administration

  • Network Security and Management

  • CI/CD Pipelines, Release Management, and Automation

  • Containerization Solutions: Orchestration and migration from monolithic architectures to modern containerized systems

  • Kubernetes Administration and Development

  • Public Key Infrastructure (PKI) management

Our broad spectrum of expertise fuels an array of specialized DevOps projects:

  • Developer Tooling and Automation: Boost developer productivity by leveraging automation tools and industry best practices.

  • User and Identity Management Solutions: Enhance security with Single Sign-On (SSO), Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA), Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), and Keycloak implementation.

  • Performance Tuning, Optimization, and Analysis: Identify and rectify performance bottlenecks, improving overall system efficiency and effectiveness.
