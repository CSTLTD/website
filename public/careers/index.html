Careers at CST

Join Our Team

At CST, we are always on the lookout for talented, passionate, and innovative individuals to join our team. We pride ourselves on fostering a creative and dynamic work environment that encourages growth, learning, and collaboration. While we may not have any open positions at the moment, we believe in the power of initiative and welcome applications from those who share our vision and values.

Open Positions

Currently, we do not have any open positions. However, this does not mean opportunities won’t arise in the future.

Why CST?

  • Innovation at Heart: We thrive on innovation and creativity, continuously seeking out new ideas and technologies to advance our work.

  • Growth Opportunities: We are committed to the professional and personal growth of our team members, offering various opportunities for learning and development.

  • Collaborative Culture: Our team is our greatest asset. We cultivate a supportive and collaborative work environment where every voice is heard.

  • Diverse Projects: Working with us means engaging in a wide range of projects, ensuring that your workday is always exciting and challenging.

Initiative Applications

We appreciate the time and effort put into initiative applications. If you believe you can make a significant contribution to our team, we’d love to hear from you! Please submit your CV and a cover letter explaining why you would like to work with us and how you believe you can contribute to our company. While we cannot guarantee an immediate opening, we will keep your application on file for future opportunities that match your skills and experience.

How to apply: Please send your application to jobs@cst-bg.net, including:

  • A detailed CV
  • A cover letter outlining your interest in CST and how you can contribute to our success
  • Any relevant portfolio or project examples (if applicable) We review initiative applications on a rolling basis and will contact you should a suitable position become available. Thank you for considering CST as your next career step. We look forward to the possibility of welcoming you to our team.
