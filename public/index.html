Professional Software Development & IT Services

CST is a professional IT service provider headquartered in Svilengrad, Bulgaria. For nearly three decades we have designed and delivered custom software, cloud & multi-cloud platforms, DevOps and infrastructure, Big Data, Blockchain, and real-time communication solutions for enterprise clients across Europe.

Our mission is to turn our clients’ vision into high-quality, functional, and reliable software — a commitment reflected in long-term partnerships with companies such as Deutsche Telekom, SAP, and T-Systems.

Selected Projects

GAIA-X Federation Services

T-Systems International · 2022–2023 — Core federation services for the European GAIA-X ecosystem — identity & trust, federated catalog, and sovereign data exchange — deployed on Kubernetes with Helm-based CI/CD.

ALTERNATIVE

EU Horizon 2020 · 2021–2024 — A cloud data platform and CKAN-based data exchange that power machine-learning cardiotoxicity research across a European consortium.

Daiteap Multi-Cloud Platform

Daiteap · 2019–2023 — A self-service orchestrator for managing Kubernetes clusters, virtual machines, and S3 storage across AWS, GCP, Azure, and edge devices.

NOMAD

Deutsche Telekom · 2018–2022 — A Hyperledger Fabric platform for signing roaming and mobile data contracts between mobile network operators worldwide.

Digital Service Assistant (ELIZA / DSA)

DataLab / HALO

Deutsche Telekom · 2015–2019 — A Big Data infrastructure-as-a-service platform on Open Telekom Cloud, automating Hadoop cluster provisioning with Kerberos, LDAP, and monitoring.
