Professional Software Development & IT Services

CST is a professional IT service provider headquartered in Svilengrad, Bulgaria. For nearly three decades we have designed and delivered custom software, cloud & multi-cloud platforms, DevOps and infrastructure, Big Data, Blockchain, and real-time communication solutions for enterprise clients across Europe.

Our mission is to turn our clients’ vision into high-quality, functional, and reliable software — a commitment reflected in long-term partnerships with companies such as Deutsche Telekom, SAP, and T-Systems.

Services

Custom Software Development

Cloud & Multi-Cloud

About CST
