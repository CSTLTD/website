CST (Computer Systems and Technologies LTD) is a software development company in Svilengrad, Bulgaria. We specialize in custom software, cloud & multi-cloud architecture, DevOps, data spaces, and blockchain engineering.

## Services

### Custom Software Development
Bespoke enterprise software, APIs, MVPs, and research prototypes — delivered end-to-end on a technology stack chosen for your project.

### DevOps as a Service
CI/CD pipelines, Infrastructure as Code, Kubernetes, and automation that improve delivery speed, security, and reliability.

### IT Consulting
Technology strategy, process optimization, modernization, and risk management across the software lifecycle.

### Data Spaces / IDSA / GAIA-X
Sovereign, compliant data-sharing platforms built on GAIA-X, IDS, and the Eclipse Dataspace Connector.

### Blockchain
Private and public blockchain solutions with Hyperledger Fabric, smart contracts, and HSM-grade key security.

### Cloud / Multi-cloud / IoT
Cloud migration, Kubernetes orchestration, and multi-cloud management across AWS, GCP, Azure, and on-premise.

## Projects

### Daiteap — Multi-Cloud Management Platform
Self-service platform for managing Kubernetes clusters, VMs, and S3 storage across AWS, GCP, Azure, and edge devices (2019–2023).

Key technologies: CKAN, JupyterHub, S3, OIDC/SSO, ML pipelines.

### GAIA-X
Core federation services for a sovereign, interoperable European data infrastructure (2022–2023).
Key technologies: Kubernetes, Helm, DID management, identity & trust.
