# Professional Software Development & IT Services

CST is a professional IT service provider headquartered in Svilengrad, Bulgaria. For nearly three decades we have designed and delivered custom software, cloud & multi-cloud platforms, DevOps and infrastructure, Big Data, Blockchain, and real-time communication solutions for enterprise clients across Europe.

Our mission is to turn our clients' vision into high-quality, functional, and reliable software — a commitment reflected in long-term partnerships with companies such as Deutsche Telekom, SAP, and T-Systems.

## Services {#services}

### Custom Software Development
End-to-end engineering of web, mobile, and enterprise applications — from requirements gathering and architecture to deployment and long-term support.

### Cloud & Multi-Cloud
Cloud architecture, migration, and Kubernetes orchestration across AWS, GCP, Azure, Alibaba Cloud, and on-premise environments.

### DevOps & Infrastructure
CI/CD automation, Infrastructure as Code (Terraform, Ansible), Kubernetes administration, monitoring, and system integration.

### Big Data & Analytics
Hadoop-based platforms and data pipelines (Cloudera, Hortonworks, Spark, Kafka) that turn large datasets into business insight.

### Blockchain & DLT
Hyperledger Fabric and Indy networks, smart contracts, self-sovereign identity (DID/SSI), and distributed ledger research.

### Data Spaces & GAIA-X
Sovereign, compliant data sharing built on GXFS, the Eclipse Dataspace Connector, and IDS/GAIA-X standards.

### WebRTC & VoIP
Real-time audio/video platforms, SIP/RTP protocol stacks, TURN/STUN infrastructure, and unified messaging solutions.

### Consulting & Outsourcing
Technology strategy, architecture consulting, and dedicated nearshore engineering teams from Bulgaria.

## About CST {#about}

Founded in 1997, CST has grown from an IT hardware and software company into a full-stack software engineering partner for enterprises across Europe — today operating from Svilengrad and Sofia, Bulgaria.

### Milestones

- **1997** — Founded as an IT hardware and software company
- **2015** — Established a Big Data & Hadoop practice
- **2016** — Opened our Sofia office
- **2018** — Established a Blockchain & DLT practice
- **2019** — Expanded into Hybrid & Multi-Cloud architecture
- **2022** — Launched the Daiteap multi-cloud platform
